About Me

Developer, game creator, and local AI enthusiast.

Background

With over 20 years of experience in transportation, I've spent the last several years pivoting into game development and local AI experimentation.

I'm the creator of Call of the Druid, a druidic, Celtic-inspired open world game for Roblox, and the founder of PetalScroll Studios.

Through Today We Learn Together (TWLT), I share local AI benchmarks, model comparisons, and practical tutorials for running AI on consumer hardware.

What I Believe

AI should be accessible to everyone — not just those with massive cloud budgets. Local models, open tools, and shared knowledge can democratize this technology.

Game development is a form of storytelling. Call of the Druid isn't about saving the world — it's about quiet wonder, nature, and connection.
