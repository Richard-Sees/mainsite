A practical path into technology.

After a long career in transportation, I began putting more of my time into game development and local AI experimentation.

I’m the creator of Call of the Druid, a Celtic- and Gallic-influenced Roblox project, and the founder of PetalScroll Studios. Through Today We Learn Together (TWLT), I share local AI comparisons, plain-language explanations, and practical lessons from consumer hardware.

The common thread is real-world problem solving: understand the constraints, test honestly, and keep learning.
